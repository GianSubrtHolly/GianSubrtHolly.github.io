Liminal
Gardens.

Overview

A quiet exploration of overgrown architecture, soft light and the stories left behind.

Liminal Gardens is a visual development project about places that have been reclaimed by time. The brief was to create an environment that felt both welcoming and slightly unknowable, using a restrained palette and strong architectural silhouettes.

I developed the key environment mood, location thumbnails, lighting studies and paintovers that established the visual direction for the wider world.
