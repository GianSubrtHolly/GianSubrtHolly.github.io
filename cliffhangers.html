Curtain & flag assets

Environmental art

Created optimised curtain and flag assets with Marvelous Designer, then remeshed them in Blender.

Procedural brick generator

Production decision

Built to help the environmental art team create random broken-brick walls. After discussing the workflow with the environmental artists, they preferred handcrafted walls. I agreed and scrapped the generator.

Substance Designer base material

Shared material

Created a configurable Substance Designer base material as an undercoat for the team's assets, helping them fit the painted art style. I exposed most parameters and shared it with a teammate for feedback and improvements. They created presets for the team using my material as the base.

Props, proxies, materials & tools

Props & tools

Created proxy models for all of our props so the team could use them immediately. I built a coin-pile generator for environmental art, enabling quick, optimised set dressing with more control and faster iteration than a gravity simulation. I also created a quicksand material for a trap.

Team communication & art direction

T P
Team direction

Stayed available to teammates through Microsoft Teams whenever they needed support. I made decisions about asset scale and design, then communicated them to the team. I also created an art and technical direction PowerPoint outlining the asset and texture production workflow.

Asset playground & integration

Asset integration

Integrated art assets into Unreal Engine and built an asset playground, giving team members a dedicated place to find and access them.
