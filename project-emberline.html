Emberline
in the heat.

Overview

Shapes, silhouettes and a language of heat for a world at the edge of collapse.

Emberline began with a question: how can a landscape communicate danger before a single character appears? I explored compressed compositions, hot colour accents and heavy atmospheric perspective to make the environment feel active and unstable.

The final direction brings together environment keys, prop callouts and a visual library for a team to build from.
